Thank you for installing AttendSmart!
AttendSmart is a software tool designed to help companies manage customer queues efficiently while enhancing the customer experience.

INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS:
Step 1: Ensure your system meets the requirements for AttendSmart. (Windows 10 or later, macOS 14 or later, at least 1 GB of storage available)
Step 2: Press the download button.
Step 3: (If your file downloads in a .zip folder, skip to step 3.5) Once downloaded, open AttendSmart.exe
Step 3.5: (For the .zip folder) Right click the AttendSmart.zip folder and select extract. You can extract this wherever you would like. Once extracted, open AttendSmart.exe.

You have completed the AttendSmart installation tutorial.
